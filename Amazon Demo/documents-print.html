Carrier Terms of Service

Neutral training copy for document workflow demonstration.

1. Scope

This document defines the service terms between the platform and the carrier for operational workflows, communications, and settlement handling.

2. Service Responsibilities

  • The carrier maintains active compliance records and operational readiness.
  • The platform provides access to available work opportunities and account tools.
  • Both parties maintain accurate records for assigned and completed work.

3. Safety and Compliance

  • The carrier follows all applicable safety standards for each assignment.
  • Required certifications and insurance categories stay current in account records.
  • Policy updates may be issued and acknowledged through the document center.

4. Settlement and Disputes

Settlement details are provided through account tools. Disputes are submitted through designated support and payments channels.

5. Data Handling

Operational data is used to support dispatch workflows, account management, and performance reporting. Confidential information must be protected by both parties.

6. Changes and Termination

Terms may be revised by policy notice. Continued service use indicates acceptance of current terms.
