Auctions message

Auctions are for future loads, so even if we let you place a bid, you would not know whether you will win it or exactly how those loads will come in because they are scheduled weeks ahead. If you start working at a company, they also will not let you book Auctions right away. Learning how to work with spot loads, the simpler Load Board, and Post a Truck is enough to get you started. Auctions and Contracts will come with experience.
